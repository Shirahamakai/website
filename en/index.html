The problem

Three places where guests are lost

1. Not found on Google

Guests search in English. If your Google listing has no English name, few photos and thin details, your inn simply does not appear.

2. Not listed — or listed thinly — on global OTAs

Without a proper page on Booking.com or Expedia, guests who are ready to book pass by without ever seeing you.

3. No English information

No English page, and no replies to English reviews. Guests cannot check the details, feel unsure, and give up.

What this can cost

¥15,000 average spend per group × 2 groups a month × 12 months = ¥360,000 a year.

An estimate, based on the assumptions shown. But if even a few bookings a month are being lost, the yearly gap grows quickly — while fixing the setup is a one-time job.

FAQ

Questions we are asked

Can I take only the free assessment?

Yes. Many inns start there. There is no obligation to order anything afterwards.

Do you take bookings or handle payments?

No. We prepare information and listings only. Bookings and payments stay between you and your guests, as they are now.

How long does the work take?

Two to four weeks, depending on the pack. We confirm the schedule before we start.

Who owns the accounts and listings?

You do. Everything is set up in your own Google and OTA accounts and handed over to you.

Do you guarantee more bookings?

No — and we will not pretend to. What we guarantee is the setup itself: being found, being understood, and being bookable.

I don't speak English. Is that a problem?

No. We handle the English. You only confirm the facts about your inn.

Which areas do you cover?

The Nanki area of Wakayama — Shirahama, Tanabe and the Kumano Kodo area first. Please ask if you are nearby.
