KUMANO KODO · ACCESS

How to get to the Kumano Kodo from Osaka or Kyoto

Choose your trailhead before buying a railway ticket.

By Shirahama Days editorial team · Sources checked 19 November 2026 · Desk-researched guide

Quick answer: For the western Nakahechi gateway, travel to Kii-Tanabe by the Kuroshio limited express and plan your onward bus separately.

Compare the railway gateways

StartGatewayIndicative train time
Shin-OsakaKii-TanabeAbout 2 hours
TennojiKii-TanabeAbout 1 hour 45 minutes
KyotoKii-TanabeAbout 2 hours 35 minutes on a suitable direct service

These are tourism bureau estimates, not guaranteed journey times. Some Kyoto departures require a change at Shin-Osaka. Choose Kii-Tanabe rather than an unrelated Tanabe station.

Connect to your actual trailhead

Kii-Tanabe is a gateway, not the beginning of every walking route. For a Nakahechi start at Takijiri-oji, check the official bus timetable and the correct stop. Allow time to transfer, get supplies and account for delays. An overnight in Tanabe may be preferable to beginning a walk late.

Before departure

  • Confirm the train and onward bus for the exact travel date.
  • Confirm your first night's bed and check-in deadline.
  • Arrange luggage separately if necessary.
  • Choose a walking section suited to the weather, daylight and your ability.

Check luggage transfer options and what to do if accommodation is unavailable.

Plan the return differently

Instead of immediately returning to Osaka, consider a coastal stay. Shirahama has beaches and hot springs; plan its onward transport using our Kumano to Shirahama guide.

Sources and updates
