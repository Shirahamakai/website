Kumano Kodo accommodation sold out? How to replan your walk

Keep the pilgrimage possible without forcing an unrealistic walking day.

By Shirahama Days editorial team · Sources checked 19 November 2026 · Desk-researched guide

Quick answer: Try alternative dates or nearby overnight bases with confirmed transport, then rebuild the walking itinerary around confirmed beds.

The Tanabe City Kumano Tourism Bureau identifies limited accommodation in smaller settlements and suggests using buses and larger overnight bases. Availability is never guaranteed.

Check bookings before changing plans

KUMANO TRAVEL requests are not instantly guaranteed: wait for confirmation and payment instructions. Before cancelling a confirmed room, check the replacement and cancellation rules with your provider. Separate operators may have different policies.

Rebuild your walking day

Choose the trail section first, then check outward and return buses, daylight, fitness, weather and the time your accommodation expects you. Do not extend a mountain walk simply because the next village is full. Carry essentials and consult the official route guidance.

Official bus information.

When a coastal extension makes sense

After completing a suitable walk, stay in Tanabe, Katsuura or Shirahama for a rest. Shirahama is a separate destination rather than an automatic substitute for remote trail accommodation. Compare the onward journey in our Kumano to Shirahama guide and luggage guide.
