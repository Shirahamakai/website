SHIRAHAMA · ACCOMMODATION

Where to stay in Shirahama: beach, onsen or station?

Pick the neighbourhood that matches your holiday, rather than simply the cheapest room.

By Shirahama Days editorial team · Sources checked 25 November 2026 · Desk-researched guide

Quick answer: Your arrival time, your departure time and your next destination matter more than the neighbourhood. If you arrive late, check the last train or bus you can actually use on your travel date, and the property's reception and dinner hours. If you leave early, check the first connection you need the next morning. A property near the station or on a bus route can make this easier, but only if those connections work for your actual property and dates. If you can reach your room comfortably before dinner and want a slower day, a second night in the same place gives you room to choose between the beach and an onsen. If getting to Shirahama would eat into your rest time, compare it with staying where you finish walking or where you go next.

Plan your nights first

Write down three things before you compare hotels: where you finish walking, when you can arrive, and where you need to be the next day. Then use the table below as a starting point. These are editorial suggestions for planning, not measured travel times.

Two nights are not right for everyone. If your onward plan is fixed, one well-planned night can be more restful than two rushed ones. Changing hotels between the two nights is a separate decision: it means packing and moving your bags again, so do it only if you have a specific reason, such as a bath or room type you want to try. When Shirahama may not suit you

  • You need to be at a fixed place early the next morning and the connection from Shirahama is long.
  • Your next destination lies further along the Kumano side, and the detour would use up the rest you were hoping to gain.
  • You want a quiet mountain village stay rather than a developed seaside resort town.

The prefectural tourism itinerary notes that the coastal area, where most of the hotels are located, is a short bus or taxi ride from the station. A seaside hotel is therefore not automatically close to the station, and a station hotel is not automatically close to the beach.

Summer versus winter

In summer, prioritise access to the beach, a place to change and shade. In winter, check the accommodation's bathing arrangements, whether there are private baths, and whether dinner is available on site. A sea view does not guarantee a private onsen.

Questions to ask before booking

  • Is dinner included, and what is the latest arrival time?
  • Is the bath shared or private, and what are its access rules?
  • Does the property offer a station shuttle or luggage delivery? Is a reservation needed?
  • Where is check-in? Some properties check guests in at a front desk in a different building from the rooms.
  • Can you wash and dry clothes? Ask whether the property has a washing machine or dryer for guests, when you can use it, what it costs and how long drying takes.
  • Are there stairs or accessibility limitations?
  • What are the cancellation conditions?

Check directly with the property. Conditions differ, and that includes laundry: after a multi-day walk you may want clean, dry clothes, so ask before you book rather than assume. For example, the official site of INFINITO HOTEL & SPA lists a station shuttle that you reserve in advance, check-in from 15:00 to 21:00, and a 19:30 limit when dinner is included. Another example: the official site of SEAMORE RESIDENCE asks guests to check in at the front desk of Shirahama Key Terrace Hotel Seamore and then move to the residence where they will stay. Those details apply to those properties and to the information shown on their sites at the time we checked, so confirm them for your own dates and plan. The prefectural itinerary also notes that dinner at a ryokan can be served early, around 6 p.m., so arriving late may mean missing it. These are examples, not a ranking or a commercial partnership. We do not claim live prices, availability, a verified tattoo policy or laundry facilities at any property.

Make the most of your time

If you arrive after walking in Kumano, keep the first evening relaxed. If you have a second night, use the middle day for one main activity rather than several. Buses from JR Shirahama Station run towards the Shirarahama, Engetsuto and Sandanbeki area; check the Meiko Bus page for services from Shirahama Station for your date and drop-off stop.
