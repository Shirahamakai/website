Minakata Kumagusu Museum: discover the natural history behind Shirahama

After walking through the forests of Kumano, you might find yourself wondering about the plants, fungi and landscapes you passed along the way. A visit to the Minakata Kumagusu Museum offers a different way to continue that journey on Wakayama's coast.

By Shirahama Days editorial team · Sources checked 22 November 2026 · Desk-researched guide

Quick answer: Check the museum calendar, allow for the steep approach from the bus stop, and leave time to rest afterwards.

Who was Minakata Kumagusu?

Minakata Kumagusu was a naturalist whose interests extended across biology, folklore and local history. The museum in Shirahama preserves material from his studies, including notebooks and specimens. The regional tourism bureau also reports English explanations and multilingual audio guides; confirm availability with the museum.

Plan your visit

According to the museum's own visitor FAQ, checked on 22 November 2026:

  • Opening: 09:00–17:00, final admission at 16:30.
  • Admission: ¥700 for high-school-age visitors and adults; ¥300 for primary and junior-high-school pupils; younger children free.
  • Normally closed on Thursdays, with holiday exceptions and a year-end closure period. The museum's pages differ on exact year-end dates, so check its calendar before visiting.
  • The museum lists several cashless payment options, but availability can change.

Use the museum's official information to confirm the latest arrangements. The prefectural listing gives a different admission price, so this guide follows the venue's own FAQ.

Make room for an unhurried afternoon

Rather than combining every coastal attraction in one rushed day, consider visiting the museum, then leaving time for a meal and a rest at your accommodation. The museum FAQ lists Rinkai bus stop, followed by about 600 metres on foot, including a steep slope. Allow for that climb after your hike and confirm the bus for your date. The museum visit does not require access to Kashima Island, and this guide does not propose landing there.
