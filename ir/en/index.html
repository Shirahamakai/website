Future plans are not a current booking option

The planned integrated resort on Yumeshima combines hotels, conference and exhibition facilities, dining, entertainment and a casino. MGM Osaka is the operator. The opening target is a plan, not a promise for a particular travel date.

The Osaka Convention & Tourism Bureau also describes a future transport and tourism hub connecting the resort with other regions. That does not establish a confirmed transfer, package or booking service to Shirahama. We do not offer IR tickets, resort reservations or a partnership with the operator.

For travel before opening, choose from places and services operating now. If your trip depends on a future facility, check the operator's latest announcement before committing to it.

Sources: Osaka Prefecture FAQ, MGM Osaka and Osaka Convention & Tourism Bureau, checked 22 November 2026.
