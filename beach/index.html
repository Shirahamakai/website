Shirarahama Beach, Shirahama Town, Wakayama. About 15 minutes by bus from JR Shirahama Station. Swimming hours: 9:00–17:00, with lifeguards on duty.

For our international guests

Shirarahama is Japan's famous white-sand beach — the only sister beach of Waikiki in the world, with sand imported from Australia. Our beach house (opening summer 2027) rents umbrellas and swim rings, and offers lockers, showers, and simple beach food. Prices are posted in English, staff can help you in English, and cards are accepted. If you are walking the Kumano Kodo, this is where the trail day ends: in the sea.

Online booking for beach sets (umbrella + swim rings + shaved ice) will be available on GetYourGuide and Viator before the season opens.
